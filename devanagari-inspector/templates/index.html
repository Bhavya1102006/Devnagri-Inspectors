THE METHOD

How it works

  1. 01
    Preprocess

    Normalize Roman text and separate punctuation.

  2. 02
    Script detection

    Keep words already written in Devanagari.

  3. 03
    Dictionary check

    Match canonical words and known variants.

  4. 04
    Transliteration

    Convert unmatched words with the ITRANS scheme.

  5. 05
    Suggestion

    Rank nearby dictionary spellings for review.

  6. 06
    Output

    Combine the selected Devanagari word forms.
